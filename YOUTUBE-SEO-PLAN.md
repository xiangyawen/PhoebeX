# Phoebe YouTube → Website Growth Plan

Prepared: 2026-11-15. These are drafts only: do not upload or publish until the channel owner approves the final video, title, description and thumbnail.

## Audience and purpose

- First-time buyers and Mandarin/Cantonese-speaking households considering Greater Vancouver or Fraser Valley.
- Each video answers one decision question, then sends viewers to one matching bilingual guide.
- Use the spoken language that suits the finished video; keep the English search title and add Mandarin/Cantonese captions where available.

- Working title: `First-Time Home Buyer Vancouver: 5 Costs to Plan Before You View`
- Viewer promise: Explain the costs and questions to list before a buyer starts viewings; do not quote personalized lending or tax outcomes.

- Suggested tags: `first time home buyer canada`, `vancouver real estate`, `vancouver realtor`, `home buying process canada`
