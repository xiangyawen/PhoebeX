Buying in Richmond: plan the everyday, then choose the home.

Think about the journeys and spaces you use every week. Then compare a condo, townhouse or detached home against that routine.

How should you begin a Richmond home search?

Start with work and daily destinations, your preferred home type and total costs. Compare exact addresses for access, light, privacy and maintenance responsibilities. A park visit or a convenient station is one part of the decision, not proof that a whole neighbourhood will suit you.

Condo, townhouse or detached home?

Condo

Check the unit’s layout, noise, storage and parking together with building condition, fees and strata documents. Walk the route from the unit to the places you need.

Townhouse

Try the stairs and room arrangement as you would use them daily. Compare outdoor space and parking with strata responsibilities, planned work and fees.

Detached home

Consider the building and land together: access, yard work, drainage, major equipment and previous alterations. Research the actual property and discuss suitable inspections.

A consistent comparison

Use the same budget and daily needs for each type. Extra floor area is not useful if the layout or ongoing work does not suit you.

How does transit fit your routine?

TransLink describes the Canada Line as linking Downtown Vancouver, Richmond and YVR. Check the current SkyTrain information and plan the full journey from each home, including walking or bus connections. Do not assume every Richmond address is within an easy walk of a station.

Also visit at the times you would normally travel. Observe street noise, access and the surroundings in person rather than relying on a listing’s broad location label.

Terra Nova: a local-life example from Phoebe’s notes

Phoebe’s July 27, 2025 post names Terra Nova Adventure Play Environment. The City of Richmond’s official park page provides the place to check its features and visitor information. Use a visit to think about which parks and activities your household would actually use.

Her post is a park recommendation, not a complete account of living or buying in every Richmond community. This guide’s home-search questions are added separately to help structure your own research.

What should you verify before choosing?

  1. Compare financing, closing costs and ongoing expenses for the exact home.
  2. For strata property, review current documents, budgets and planned work; BCFSA’s Form B information is one reference.
  3. Confirm intended uses, alterations and any address-specific planning or hazard questions with the City and relevant professionals.
  4. Check school or childcare arrangements directly, rather than assuming admission from a listing.
  5. Discuss inspections and unresolved issues before making commitments. Read BCFSA’s inspection guidance.

Richmond buyer questions

Is this a current Richmond price guide?

No. It is a practical home-search guide. Ask for current available properties and comparable sales within your budget.

Does access to Canada Line make every address convenient?

No. Test the entire trip from the actual home at the time you need to travel.

Can Phoebe help me compare Richmond with the Fraser Valley?

Yes. Start with your work location and budget, then use the Fraser Valley guide and first-time buyer checklist to compare options consistently.
