Buying a home in Vancouver in Mandarin, Cantonese or English

Choose the language that helps your household ask clear questions. Then use the same budget, viewing and offer checklist for every home you compare.

What does bilingual home-buying support help with?

It gives your household space to discuss priorities in Mandarin, Cantonese or English before a decision needs to be made. Use that conversation to make a shared list of budget limits, daily journeys, home features and questions for specialists. Support in a preferred language does not change the rules of a transaction or replace legal, lending, tax or immigration advice.

What should your household prepare before viewing homes?

  1. One monthly budget. Include mortgage payments, property taxes, insurance, utilities, strata fees where applicable, moving and a reserve.
  2. One priorities list. Agree on work and school journeys, bedroom needs, accessibility, parking, outdoor space and timing.
  3. Questions for your lender. Mortgage preapproval is useful planning information, but it is not final approval for a specific home. FCAC explains the distinction.

What should you ask at a viewing?

Walk through the home as if it were an ordinary weekday. Notice daylight where you would work or study, traffic and other sound, storage, visible drainage and the route to places you use. Record questions instead of treating a quick visit as a professional inspection. The BCFSA inspection guide explains why inspection decisions deserve careful consideration.

Questions to settle before an offer

Can we review priorities in Cantonese, Mandarin or English?

Yes. Phoebe provides home-search support in all three languages. Put key questions and your household’s non-negotiables in writing so everyone is working from the same list.

Does bilingual support replace a lawyer, lender or accountant?

No. Ask each qualified professional to explain the parts of the transaction they are responsible for, and confirm advice against your own circumstances.

What should I understand about a buyer agency agreement?

Read the services, term and payment arrangements before you sign. BCFSA's guide explains the agreement and why it matters.

After your household agrees on the basics, compare Richmond, Langley, Mission and Maple Ridge using the same list. For the full first-time process, read the Greater Vancouver first-time buyer guide.
