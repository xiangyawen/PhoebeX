How do you choose where to buy in the Fraser Valley?

Start with the places you need to reach, a budget you can comfortably carry and the kind of home you can maintain. Compare each area against the same priorities so you can see the trade-offs between price, space and everyday travel.

Four areas to start your comparison

Mission

Compare parks, everyday amenities and the commute from each home. If you travel into Vancouver, check train times and the journey to the station. Read the Mission guide.

Abbotsford

When exploring East Abbotsford and other areas, look beyond the view. Check the home’s condition, access, school arrangements and property records. Read the Abbotsford guide.

Langley

Compare Walnut Grove and Willoughby by the age, layout and condition of the homes you shortlist. For townhouses, include strata fees, planned work and long-term upkeep.

Chilliwack

Explore Sardis and Promontory with space, slope and access in mind. Test trips to work, shops and activities before deciding whether a home fits your routine.

Older townhouse or newer home: what should you compare?

Two townhouses with similar asking prices can cost different amounts to own. Compare strata fees, reserve funding and planned maintenance, then try the layout as you would use it each day.

  1. Compare current fees, the operating budget and reserve funding.
  2. Read available depreciation reports, meeting minutes and information about planned work or levies.
  3. Obtain and review the applicable strata documents. BCFSA’s Form B resource explains the role of a current Information Certificate.
  4. Compare the age and condition of roofs, windows and equipment with the actual maintenance record.
  5. Walk the layout as you would use it: stairs, bathrooms, parking, daylight and storage.

A newer home is not a guarantee of ten maintenance-free years, and an older home does not automatically mean a major levy. Ask questions about the particular property.

Make commuting part of the price comparison

If you plan to take the train from Mission, check West Coast Express times and direction of service first. For any area, try the full journey to work and back, including parking and connections. Consider whether the schedule fits the hours you need to travel.

If you are moving provinces, plan your work arrangements, temporary accommodation and viewing trip early. If possible, spend time in the areas you are considering and try your normal routine before narrowing the shortlist.

Fraser Valley buying questions

Will moving farther east always save money?

Not necessarily. Add travel and maintenance to the purchase and ownership costs you are comparing. Use current listings and comparable sales to work out what your budget buys.

Does one school ranking tell me where to buy?

No. Confirm catchments, programs and enrolment requirements directly with the school district, and compare the needs of your household.

What does Fraser Valley mean on this page?

This is a practical housing-search comparison, not an administrative-boundary definition. The article names each city explicitly; municipal boundaries and real-estate statistical areas are not interchangeable.
