Can you commute from Mission to Downtown Vancouver?

Possibly—but only if the train times, station trip and return journey work for your actual week. Test the complete door-to-door trip before choosing a home.

Short answer

West Coast Express runs between Mission City and Waterfront on weekdays at peak times. It goes west to Downtown Vancouver in the morning and east to Mission in the afternoon and evening. That makes it useful for some regular office schedules, but it is not an all-day or weekend rail service.

What should you test before buying in Mission?

  1. Your exact train. Check the official timetable for both directions, including holiday or reduced-service notices. Do not choose a home on the assumption that a later train will be available.
  2. Door to station. Drive, walk or take the feeder route from the specific address at the time you would leave. Mission City Station parking, drop-off and accessibility are practical parts of the commute.
  3. Waterfront to work. Add the walk, bus or SkyTrain connection after arrival. A train arrival time is not your office arrival time.
  4. The trip home. Test a realistic late meeting, appointment or missed train scenario. Decide in advance what your alternative would be.
  5. Weekly cost. Check the current WCE fare and parking information directly with TransLink; fares and service notices can change.

Use this alongside the Mission home buying and living guide. A commute is only one part of whether the neighbourhood and home fit your routine.

Common questions

Does West Coast Express run on weekends?

Its regular service is a weekday peak-period commuter service. Check TransLink before relying on it for a particular date, especially holidays and special events.

How long is the Mission City to Waterfront train trip?

Scheduled times vary by train. Compare the current departure and arrival times on TransLink’s timetable, then add your trip to and from each station.
